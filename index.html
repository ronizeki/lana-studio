About the studio

A Batam studio built for brands that refuse to blend in.

Lana Studio is a creative agency based at Komp. Palm Spring in Taman Baloi, Batam. We sit at the point where strategy meets production — planning the campaign, designing the identity, building the event, and placing the media that carries it.

That range matters here. In a market where national brands compete for the same mall atrium and the same stretch of roadside, the difference is rarely the budget. It's whether the idea is sharp enough to stop someone walking past.

  • 01Strategy before aesthetics. Every campaign starts with the objective, the audience and the one message that has to land.
  • 02Production in-house. Creative design, motion, event build and media placement under one roof — fewer handoffs, tighter deadlines.
  • 03Local fluency. We know Batam's malls, roads, audiences and media formats because we work in them every week.
